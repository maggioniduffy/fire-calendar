## Design

- Counts, filters, 2.5 degree blocks, metrics (relative bias = sum(pred)/sum(obs) - 1; WAPE = sum|pred - obs| / sum(obs)), the SA2.2 estimator with `k` selected by the inner leave-one-year-out, and the conformal intervals are those of `reports/02/baselines.md`. Day and night are fitted separately and summed. Block-year = sum over the months of a test year of one block. All cubes are on the 70 blocks of `sf-2026.11.1` (detections in other blocks dropped: 0).
- Chain: NOAA-20 -> S-NPP (months from 2018-05) then S-NPP -> MODIS (months from 2012-02), each leg with its own `k`; the route factor is the product per month x block x day/night. Direct: one NOAA-20 -> MODIS factor, same estimator and `k` selection, months from 2018-05.
- Forward holdouts (P2): cut-offs end of 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024; every leg and `k` refitted on months up to the cut-off, no gap; test every full calendar year after it up to 2025 (10 cut-off x test-year cells) and, apart, the 2026 block of complete satellite-months. Pooled = sum|err| / sum(obs) over all (cut-off, test year, block) rows. The 2026 block never enters the P5 decision.
- Conformal bands per cut-off: residuals of the inner leave-one-year-out (6-month gap) on months up to the cut-off, every leg out-of-fold with its own `k`; coverage rows are those with source (NOAA-20) > 0, pooled.

## Deviations

- deviation (2026-11-15): NOAA-21 pairs: the S-NPP / NOAA-20 side of 2023-2025 comes from the FIRMS country files and 2026 from the area API, not both sides from the same API path as written in step 3 of the completion plan. Reason: P7 passed (`reports/01/SA1.6_clip.md`): the API-SP 2025 clip matches the country files for S-NPP and NOAA-20 within tolerance. NOAA-21 has no country files (all of it is API, SP), so its clip stays unverified.
- deviation (2026-11-15): The direct route's conformal residuals re-select `k` inside every inner fold (as the chain's legs do), instead of reusing the outer fold's `k` as `fit_calibration` does for the older pairs. Reason: Revision 1 P5 asks for every factor of the route out-of-fold with its own `k`; chain and direct are then treated alike. The older pairs of `.1` are untouched.
- deviation (2026-11-15): The 2026 block (P2) and the frozen-artifact check (P9) score the complete satellite-months only (P8), which for the NOAA-20 -> MODIS route are 2026-01..04 (Terra is partial in 2026-05, -06 and -07) and for NOAA-20 -> S-NPP 2026-01..06 (S-NPP is partial in 2026-07), not 2026-01..07. Reason: P8 gates the satellite-months from 2026-01 on; no month was substituted.
- deviation (2026-11-15): The paired bootstrap resamples the rows of the (cut-off x test-year) block-year matrix and the blocks separately, since forward test years are not independent of the cut-off. Reason: P2 says 'years and blocks resampled separately'; here the first resampling unit is the cell (10 cells). Seed 20261114, 1000 resamples, 95 % percentile interval, as pre-registered.
- deviation (2026-11-15): For the chain route pair `k` is stored as nan (each leg has its own `k`, kept in the `legs` pairs of the same calibration). Reason: The calibration schema has one `k` per pair; the contract with the series code adds `route` and `legs`.

## Unverified

- Observed MODIS from 2022 is in the drift epoch (Terra 2023+, Aqua 2024+ by P3, `reports/02/SA2.6.md`); the cause (orbit drift) is unverified. MODIS-eq means 'MODIS as in the calibration period' (2012-2025), so every forward score here, and the 2026 check, measure the factor against a drifting MODIS.
- The clip of NOAA-21 (area API to Argentina) has no country file to compare with: unverified.
- Only standard-processing (SP) months enter any fit or score here. NRT months are never used; the NRT vs SP processing difference is unverified.
- Degradation with the horizon is claimed only within the same test year (the horizon is confounded with the calendar year and with MODIS drift).
- The coverage of the conformal bands is measured, not guaranteed: outside 75-85 % it is labelled unverified and the band is not widened.
- Historical satellite-months below 90 % of the median number of detection days (P8 diagnostic) stay in the primary results; the secondary table leaves them out of the test months only.
- The factors are the same for every year of a calendar month; they carry no information on interannual changes of the NOAA-20 / MODIS relationship beyond what each cut-off saw.
