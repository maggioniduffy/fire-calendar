# SA2.8: backward validation (train 2016+, test 2012-2015)

Generated by `firecal harmonize backward` on 2026-11-15. All numbers come from code. Source: monthly region cube from the 0.1 degree grid (default filters), 2.5 degree blocks; code `harmonize/backward.py` and `evaluate.py`.

Design (P4 condition 2 and Revision 1): the SA2.2 factor (`k` selected by the inner leave-one-year-out inside the training set) is trained on 2016-01..2025-12 with no gap and tested on each year 2012..2015 (2012 from 2012-02, the partial month 2012-01 is out). Every pair is trained twice: with all training months and without the drift years of that pair (P3 epochs; Terra only for `terra_to_viirs`). Paired bootstrap: years and blocks resampled separately, 1000 resamples, seed 20261114, 95 % percentile interval, on the pooled block-year WAPE and pooled |relative bias| (without - with). The gate is met only if neither metric is worse (point difference <= 0) and at least one has its interval entirely < 0. With only four test years the year resampling is coarse: read the intervals accordingly.

Whatever the verdict, `sf-2026.11.2` keeps the `sf-2026.11.1` pairs unchanged; a recalibration, if warranted, would go to a later version with its own report. Observed MODIS in drift years stays flagged "orbit drift (unverified cause)" in any case. This is a retrospective test of the relationship over time, not an alert.
