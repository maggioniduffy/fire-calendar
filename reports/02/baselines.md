# SA2.2 and SA2.3 MODIS-VIIRS harmonization baselines

Run: 2026-11-15, with `firecal harmonize evaluate` (tables generated by code). Source: NASA FIRMS per-country files (Argentina), standard processing (MODIS, VIIRS S-NPP and NOAA-20; the detail of each processing version is unverified).
Period of the local data: 2000-11 to 2025-12; overlap period used: 2012-02 to 2025-12.
Filters: type in [0], MODIS confidence >= 30, VIIRS class in ['nominal', 'high'] (default `FilterConfig`). Count: detections after the filter, summed from the 0.1° grid.

Everything is retrospective: it counts hot spots already detected and is not an alert system.

## Data and assumptions

- Scales: MODIS = Terra + Aqua; VIIRS = S-NPP only. NOAA-20 is not added to S-NPP: a transfer factor NOAA-20 -> S-NPP is fitted from 2018-05.
- Regions: 2.5° blocks (origin -90/-180), 70 blocks with at least one hot spot. Day and night are fitted separately and summed.
- Partial months excluded: S-NPP 2012-01, NOAA-20 2018-04, Aqua 2002-07.
- First S-NPP month in the local data: 2012-02.
- Unit: fitted on sums per block x month of the year x day/night; evaluated per block-month of each test year and per block-year total.
- Leave-one-year-out: each year is the test year once; training excludes the test year and 6 months on each side. Rolling origin: train on all earlier years, evaluate the next one (no gap).
- SA2.2: `f = (M + k * f_national) / (V + k)`; `k` is chosen inside the training set with an inner leave-one-year-out (6-month gap), minimizing the block-month WAPE, over the grid {0, 5, 20, 100, 500, 2000, 10000}.
- SA2.3: per-block quantile mapping on block-month totals (months of the year pooled); positives only; zero maps to zero; above the training maximum, constant ratio; with fewer than 12 positive months in either series the block uses the mapping of all blocks pooled.
- Metrics: relative bias = sum(pred)/sum(obs) - 1; WAPE = sum|pred-obs|/sum(obs); MAE; mean Poisson deviance (minimum predicted mean 0.001). They are block-month totals (n = blocks x test months) and block-year totals.

## Intervals

Nominal level 80%. Empirical coverage over block-months (or block-years) with a source count greater than zero (what is known at prediction time), by test year (leave-one-year-out). `coverage_any` in the Reading section uses the earlier definition (source or observed > 0). Relative width = sum of widths / sum of predictions.

- `jackknife (factor)` and `interannual dispersion` (inverse series only, block-month): factor +/- z * jackknife standard error (z = 1.28), and the 10 % and 90 % quantiles of the factors of each training year. They only describe the uncertainty of the factor, not the count noise of the test month.
- `conformal (relative residuals)` (both directions, block-month and block-year): inside the training set of each outer split the inner leave-one-year-out is run (6-month gap, with the `k` already chosen for that split) and the out-of-sample relative residuals `obs / pred` are pooled over block-months with source > 0 (`obs = 0` gives residual 0). Predictions of up to 5 hot spots (block-month) or 10 (block-year), 0 included, use an additive interval: conformal quantiles of `obs - pred` of those inner rows (threshold fixed in advance, not tuned with any test year). The rest is stratified into 5 predicted-count classes, with edges at quantiles of the out-of-sample training prediction (classes with fewer than 30 residuals are merged with the neighbor). The interval is the class `pred * [q10, q90]`, with the finite-sample conformal correction (ranks ceil(0.9 (n+1)) and floor(0.1 (n+1))). Block-year does not sum block-month bounds: it is calibrated with residuals of block-year totals from the same inner years, with its own classes. Nothing is tuned with the outer test years. The inner models use one year less of training than the outer model, which tends to widen slightly.

All direction x level combinations fall within 75-85 %: yes.

Rows with source = 0 and observed > 0 are not covered by construction (the factor has nothing to multiply): the other sensor sees hot spots where this one marked none. Their count and their share of the detections are reported above and do not enter the headline coverage, which uses only rows defined by the source, known at prediction time. Coverage by year varies more at block-year (few rows per test year). Training residuals are treated as exchangeable with those of the test year: if the relationship changes over time (for example Terra drift, unverified), the coverage of a year may fall below.

## SA2.4 and SA2.7 vs. baseline

LightGBM on top of the SA2.2 scale factor (the factor enters as an offset: `init_score = log(f_block x source + eps)`), with the same metrics, the same folds (leave-one-year-out with a 6-month gap, and rolling origin) and the same aggregation to block-month and block-year as SA2.2. Everything is retrospective. The publication criterion was fixed in `plan/02-harmonization.md` before the run and was not touched after seeing results.

### What was run

- **Cell model** (main candidate): rows of 0.1° cell x month x day/night where the **source** is active in the cell or its 3x3 neighborhood (the design said "some sensor active"; with the target observed at test time the row set would leak the target and, training only on rows with an active target, would bias upward the rows without a source; the change is an implementation decision, not a change of the criterion). Target detections in cell-months with no source in their 3x3 neighborhood are out of reach (like source = 0 in SA2.2): SA2.4 2.6 % of the target detections (2,565,798 rows), SA2.7 6.2 % of the target detections (1,671,420 rows).
- **Features** (all from the source, known at prediction time): count, FRP, FRP per detection, count of the other part of the day (day/night), day/night share, is-night, 3x3 count, source total in the block-month and month of the year. Ablations: with Terra/Aqua share (MODIS as source only: terra and aqua separately, FRP per satellite and Terra share; it does not exist in VIIRS -> MODIS, which stays the same) and with lat/lon. The main candidate is the base model without Terra/Aqua share and without lat/lon; the ablations are not candidates.
- **Offset:** smoothed source (3x3 on rows without a source) and rescaled per block-month-day/night to preserve the source total: without that rescaling the smoothing adds mass and with zero trees SA2.2 would not be reproduced. Factors of the training rows: out-of-sample (inner leave-one-year-out, with a gap); those of the test rows: fitted on the outer training set.
- **Block-month model:** same ideas on block-month x day/night rows (heavily regularized, 3-7 leaves), second candidate.
- **Objective and selection:** Poisson (Tweedie 1.2 only in the block-month model grid). Hyperparameters by block-month WAPE with inner leave-one-year-out inside each outer training set; learning rate 0.1 for the cell and 0.05 for the block, `num_threads` = 2, fixed seed, `deterministic`.
- **Grid (reduced for time):** cell: one structure (31 leaves, minimum 200 rows per leaf, L2 10) with 25/50/100 trees to choose from; block-month: 4 structures x 30/100/250 trees. The evaluation design (nested, same folds) was not reduced. The ablations reuse the configuration chosen by the base cell model in the same fold.
- **Intervals:** SA2.2 nested conformal scheme (`intervals.py`) on the LightGBM point predictions (out-of-sample residuals of the chosen configuration); no LightGBM quantile regression (optional diagnostic, not run).
- **Discarded run and leaf cap (not in the earlier design):** the first full run gave, in the SA2.7 cell model, predictions of the order of 1e18: leaves with Newton steps of tens on the log scale on rows without a source in their day/night part (offset at the `eps` floor) but with target detections. `max_delta_step = 1` was added (a cap on each leaf output, at most 0.1 on the log scale per tree with learning rate 0.1) **after** that first run, to fix a numerical failure; it applies to both directions and both models, and was not in the design fixed before running. The criterion, features, folds and grid did not change. The SA2.4 results of that run had already been seen before the fix; the cap was not chosen by measuring it against them (single value, no others tried). In addition, a guard stops the run if any prediction (outer or inner) is non-finite or exceeds 100 times the observed value.
- **Time and memory:** 31.3 min of wall-clock time for this part, one process per direction (2 threads each); summed peak memory (PSS of the processes) 2608 MB.

### Publication criterion and verdict

LightGBM is published in a direction only if the candidate meets all seven conditions (the 6th only in SA2.7): 1) leave-one-year-out block-month WAPE at least 1 point lower than SA2.2 and block-year not higher; 2) lower block-month WAPE in at least 10 of 14 test years; 3) improvement of the same sign under rolling origin; 4) total |bias| not worse than SA2.2's + 0.5 points and the worst year not worse; 5) block-month Poisson deviance not worse; 6) block-month conformal coverage 80 +/- 5 % and relative width not larger; 7) the paired bootstrap interval (years and blocks resampled separately, 1000 resamples, seed 20261114, 95 % percentiles) of the block-month WAPE difference excludes zero. Interpretations fixed before the run: the "worst year" is a leave-one-year-out test year; coverage and width are the block-month ones (block-year is reported, not a condition); "excludes zero" requires an upper limit < 0. The verdict of a direction is that of the main candidate (cell); the block-month model has its own, separately.

### Notes on the fit and the bias

- Trees at the lower edge of the grid (25), cell model, leave-one-year-out: SA2.7 10 of 14 folds; SA2.4 0 of 14 folds. If the choice falls on the edge, the grid may have been too short toward fewer trees.
- Zero trees: the check table gives the actual number. The `eps` floor of the offset adds, as an upper bound, eps x cell rows = SA2.4 2,566 detections over the whole period (all rows, test and training years); SA2.7 1,671 detections over the whole period (all rows, test and training years), against the SA2.2 block totals; this is the cause of the difference from the ~1 % of the design in small blocks. `eps` was not touched after seeing test results.
- The cell model bias may be structural (**unverified**): it is trained with the targets inside the candidate rows but evaluated against all targets, and between 2.6 % (SA2.4) and 6.2 % (SA2.7) of the target detections fall outside the rows. It is a possible explanation and does not change the verdict.
