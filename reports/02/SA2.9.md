## Data

- **Product:** MCD64A1 Collection 6.1 (version 061), MODIS/Terra+Aqua Burned Area Monthly L3 Global 500 m SIN grid, layer `Burn Date`; DOI 10.5067/MODIS/MCD64A1.061; archive: NASA LP DAAC. Granules found with NASA CMR (`short_name=MCD64A1`, `version=061`, bounding box of the ADM2 boundaries) and downloaded from `data.lpdaac.earthdatacloud.nasa.gov`.
- **Period:** 2000-11 to 2025-12 (302 months with data). The product starts in 2000-11; January-October 2000 do not exist.
- **Tiles and granules:** 3,020 granules, 10 MODIS tiles (h11-h14, v11-v14), sha256 and size of every file in `data/raw/mcd64a1/manifest.json`; failed or missing granules: 0. `data/` is not in git.
- **Gridding:** pixel centres from the MODIS sinusoidal grid formula (sphere radius 6,371,007.181 m, tile 1,111,950.52 m, 2,400 x 2,400 pixels, pixel 463.31 m), counted per 0.1 degree cell with the same `cell_ids` as the FIRMS grid, month taken from the granule, clipped to the union of the geoBoundaries ADM2 polygons (simplified geometry, same as the FIRMS area clip). **Pixel area used: 0.2147 km2** (the sinusoidal grid is equal-area, so every pixel has this area; the product's "500 m" is 463.3 m). Pixels with burn date 1-366 count as burned; -1 (unmapped, insufficient data) is kept as `unmapped_px`; -2 (water) and 0 are not counted. Output `data/interim/burned/mcd64a1_0.1.parquet`: 640,129 cell-months with burned area or unmapped pixels, sha256 `70266fe6394a0672...`.
- **Burned area in Argentina (clipped):** 19,103 to 74,531 km2 per year over 2000-2025 (partial years: 2000 starts in November); total 957,233 km2. Not compared with an independent figure (unverified).
- **Cell-months where MODIS flagged nothing:** 14.4 % of the burned area (137,871 of 957,233 km2, months with Terra or Aqua data) lies in cell-months with no Terra or Aqua hot spot in the same 0.1 degree cell. This is the share the variable could in principle add information about; it says nothing yet about whether SA2.7 improves.
- **Licence:** the CMR collection record (read 2026-11-15) lists access constraints "None" and no use constraints; the citation is Giglio, Justice, Boschetti and Roy, MODIS/Terra+Aqua Burned Area Monthly L3 Global 500m SIN Grid V061, NASA LP DAAC, DOI 10.5067/MODIS/MCD64A1.061. The LP DAAC / NASA data-use policy text was not read here, so the licence terms are **unverified**; access to the files needs a free NASA Earthdata login. Cite the DOI if the data are redistributed or shown.
- **Months without any granule:** none within the period.
- **Tiles per month:** every month has all 10 tiles.
- **Unmapped pixels** (`-1`, insufficient observations): 7,365,507 km2-months in total inside Argentina; they are not used as a feature.

## What was run

- **Variable** (km2, both from the 0.1 degree grid built here): `ba_cell` = MCD64A1 burned area in the cell and `ba_n3` = in the cell and its 8 neighbours, for the same calendar month. It does not depend on the sensors, exists from 2000-11 (it is available in 2000-2011, MODIS only) and is zero where no burned pixel was mapped.
- **Everything else identical** to the SA2.7 cell model of `baselines.md`: candidate rows (source active in the cell or its 3x3), offset `log(f x source + eps)`, features (9 base features), grid (one Poisson structure, 25/50/100 trees), `max_delta_step = 1`, folds (leave-one-year-out with a 6-month gap and rolling origin), seeds, nested conformal intervals, metrics and the seven-condition criterion. Only the two columns above are added in the "with" run; the block-month model and the ablations are not rerun (the variable is a cell feature).
- **Candidate rows do not change:** a burned cell without any source detection in its 3x3 is still not a row, so the variable cannot reach fires the source did not flag anywhere nearby; it only informs rows that already exist. Share of the burned area of the SA2.7 months that falls in a candidate cell-month: 94.7 % (412,471 of 435,572 km2).
- **Time and memory:** without 21.3 min, with 23.1 min of wall-clock time (run as parallel processes, 2 threads each); peak summed PSS 1477 MB.

## Limits and open items

- The candidate rows of the cell model are those with source activity in the 3x3 neighbourhood; burned area that is far from any source detection is not a row and therefore cannot be predicted. Opening the row set to burned cells would change the training rows and the unreachable share, and with it the comparison; it was not done here (it would need its own criterion fixed beforehand).
- The variable is MODIS-derived; the algorithm also uses MODIS active-fire detections to guide the mapping (from the product description, not re-read here: unverified), so it is probably not independent of the MODIS hot spots. It exists in 2000-2011, the MODIS-only period.
- Burned area is a monthly, cell-level amount while the target is counts of detections: a single large burn can sit in cells whose count is small. No Landsat or other burned-area product was used (SA0.8 access unconfirmed).
- Target detections with no source in their 3x3 (out of reach of the model, unchanged by the variable): 6.2 %. The with and without runs use the same seed, folds and hyperparameter grid, so differences come from the two added columns; with one seed and 14 test years, differences below the bootstrap interval are not distinguishable from noise.
