Days with at least one detection are counted before any filter on the rows of the source the grid uses for that month (country-sp > api-sp > nrt, never summed).

Rule: `complete` = month closed, source window end >= last day of the month and days with detections >= 90 % of the median for that calendar month and satellite over the reference period (VIIRS 2012-2025, MODIS 2003-2025; the first partial month of each record excluded). NOAA-21 has no reference before 2023-03, so its reference is 2-3 years per calendar month (thin; see `ref_n`) and its SP comes from the area API only (no country files).
