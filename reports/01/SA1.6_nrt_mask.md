Generated by `firecal firms nrt-mask` (`firecal.stages.grid.nrt_mask`).

Scope: this validates the `type` proxy only (all-type count minus masked cells vs the `type == 0` count, same confidence filters). NRT rows have no `type`; whether NRT and SP differ in their processing (detections, confidence, FRP) is **unverified** and is not tested here.

Source: FIRMS SP detections under data/interim/firms/detections (modis api-sp 2025-2026; modis country-sp 2000-2025; viirs-jpss1 api-sp 2025-2026; viirs-jpss1 country-sp 2018-2025; viirs-jpss2 api-sp 2023-2026; viirs-snpp api-sp 2025-2026; viirs-snpp country-sp 2012-2025).
Periods: selection = history <= 2023, scored on SP 2024; acceptance = history <= 2024, scored on SP 2025; deployment = history <= 2025. Mask = cells where `type != 0` occurs in at least k distinct years (cell 0.01 or 0.02 deg, k in [2, 3, 5]); no confidence filter on the history.

Gates (accepted per family; a family that fails keeps its NRT rows out of the grid): national bias within +-3 % per satellite x day/night; max |bias| lower than with no mask; at least 90 % of the 2.5 deg blocks with >= 100 `type == 0` detections within +-10 % block-year (family pooled).

Interpretation note: "lower |bias| than no mask" is gated on the maximum over satellite x day/night (the selection criterion); the per-cell comparison is in the tables.
