# SA1.7 FIRMS data profile (Argentina)

Run: 2026-11-14 in the cloud session, with `firecal firms download`, `ingest`, `grid` (0.1° and 0.25°) and
`profile` (tables below generated by `firecal firms profile`). Source: NASA FIRMS standard per-country files,
`https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/data/country/{modis|viirs-snpp|viirs-jpss1}/{YEAR}/..._Argentina.csv`.

## Summary

- 48 files (MODIS 2000–2025, VIIRS S-NPP 2012–2025, VIIRS NOAA-20 2018–2025), 487 MB of CSV. Detections as
  Parquet: 127 MB; 0.1° grid: 18 MB; 0.25° grid: 14 MB.
- Actual coverage: MODIS 2000-11-01 to 2025-12-31, S-NPP 2012-01-20 to 2025-12-31, NOAA-20 2018-04-01 to
  2025-12-31. The starts match the FIRMS FAQ.
- **2026 gap:** all three sensors return 404 for 2026. The standard series ends on 2025-12-31; the 2026 months
  could only come from the NRT API (SA1.6, requires `FIRMS_MAP_KEY`).
- No empty months within each product's coverage.
- One download (MODIS 2000) failed on a proxy connection reset and recovered on retry; the downloader marked it as
  `error`, not `missing`.
- 2020 is the year with the most hot spots in all three products (≈ 2.5 times 2019 in MODIS). It only describes
  counts; no cause is interpreted.
- S-NPP and NOAA-20 give almost equal counts in the years they share (2019–2025, difference < 5 %).

## Verified in the real files

- Columns equal to those assumed by the parser (MODIS with `brightness`/`bright_t31`, VIIRS with
  `bright_ti4`/`bright_ti5`; both with `type`).
- `satellite`: MODIS `Terra`/`Aqua`; VIIRS `N` (S-NPP) and `N20` (NOAA-20). VIIRS `confidence`: `l`/`n`/`h`.
- MODIS `version` changes between years: `6.2` in 2000–2017, `6.03` in 2018–2022 and `61.03` in 2023–2025. VIIRS:
  `2` in all. What each code means and whether it changes detection: **unverified**; it must be reviewed before
  harmonization (phase 2), because a processing change in 2018 or 2023 would be mistaken for a real change.


Retrospective counts of detections; not an alert system.
Filters: type in [0], MODIS confidence >= 30, VIIRS class in ['nominal', 'high'].
