# SA0.1 — NASA Space Apps 2026 rules

Consulted on 2026-10-28. Sources: [Participant FAQs](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/participant-faqs/)
(dated 13/10/2026) and [Participant Terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/).

## 1. Work before the event

- **Starting early is forbidden.** FAQ: "Teams are not allowed to begin working on the challenges prior to the
  hackathon." Site banner: "Do not begin work on your projects until the hackathon begins."
- Third-party material (code or data that is not from NASA or partner agencies) may be used if it is public or OSI
  open source, with an identified license, and every resource must be cited, even open ones (Terms, "Use of
  third-party materials").
- What counts as "work" (plan, design, own generic prior code): unverified. There is no definition in the FAQ or
  Terms; ask the Local Lead or the GO Team.

## 2. Teams and participation

- Local event: minimum 1 person (can be alone). Universal (virtual): minimum 2. Maximum 6. One team per person and
  one challenge per team.
- All ages; under-18s registered and accompanied by a parent or guardian.
- Registration open until the end of the hackathon (15/11). Participation is free.

## 3. Submission

- Through the project page on spaceappschallenge.org, by the closing time of the local event.
- Detail (video, slides, demo, repo): in the Project Submission Guide, released on 13/11. Unverified until then.
- Resources for each challenge: published on 13/11.

## 4. License and use of AI

- Original content: free or OSI-licensed; if not stated, Apache 2.0. No material under export control.
- AI ("Super Intelligence" in the official text): allowed. The submission must describe how the tools were used.
- The video is granted to NASA under a non-exclusive, perpetual license.

## 5. Judging and prizes

- Judges from NASA, partner agencies and industry. Nominees, finalists and mentions in December 2026; global
  winners in January 2027.
- Criteria: in the Judging and Awards Guide (13/11). Unverified.
- Universal event projects compete only among themselves.

## 6. CONAE challenge

- Page: <https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/modis-and-viirs-hot-spots/>
- Requirements: those of the brief (harmonized calendar in a web app, area and dates, CSV). Official resources:
  13/11.

## 7. Venues in Argentina

- Official list: <https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/>
- Córdoba 2026: unverified. Confirmed by press: Universidad de Palermo (Buenos Aires, 14/11) and Santiago del
  Estero.

According to the author (2026-10-28), they are registered in a virtual event that has a local organizer, i.e. a
virtual local event and not the Universal Event (the FAQ allows in-person or virtual local events).

- 1 person is enough for the global judging; maximum 6.
- Submission closes at the end time of that local event: confirm it on its page.
- The no-work-before-the-event rule applies all the same. Questions go to the Local Lead: Leo Ludueña (contact on

## Implications for the plan

- Developing challenge-specific code, data or design now violates the rule in point 1. Until 14/11 no challenge
  code is written, no FIRMS data is processed for it and no models are published.
- Allowed without doubt: registering, choosing a venue, forming a team, learning general tools.
- Gray zone: this plan, the clima-ideas design and its generic code (repo structure, theme). Ask the Local Lead in
  writing before using them and cite them as prior material if accepted.
- On 13/11 the submission guide, the criteria and the resources arrive: review the plan with them.
- Repo license: choose an OSI one (Apache 2.0 by default) and keep a record of AI use for the submission.
